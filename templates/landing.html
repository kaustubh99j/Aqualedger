A complete water workflow

Meter data you can act on — and prove

From the first reading to the annual report, every step is designed for households, societies and administrators.

{{ icon('readings', 'icon-lg') }}

Manual meter readings

Record cumulative meter values in cubic metres. AquaLedger automatically derives consumption and validates the meter chain.

{{ icon('chain', 'icon-lg') }}

Ethereum-backed proofs

A keccak256 digest of every reading is anchored to the AquaLedger Solidity contract. Personal details never touch the chain.

{{ icon('shield', 'icon-lg') }}

Tamper detection

Recompute each record's digest and compare it with the immutable on-chain hash. Any modified value is exposed immediately.

{{ icon('bell', 'icon-lg') }}

Limits & smart alerts

Set daily and monthly limits. AquaLedger flags threshold breaches, monthly overruns and abnormal usage spikes automatically.

{{ icon('leaf', 'icon-lg') }}

Conservation score

A transparent score compares each household's per-person usage with a 135 L/day target and tracks improvement over time.

{{ icon('chart', 'icon-lg') }}

Daily & monthly insight

Interactive Chart.js dashboards and downloadable monthly reports turn readings into decisions, not spreadsheets.

{{ icon('wallet', 'icon-lg') }}

MetaMask authorisation

Link and verify a wallet with a free signature. Authorised residents can sign their own anchoring transactions.

{{ icon('users', 'icon-lg') }}

Admin control centre

Manage residents, households, per-household limits and wallet permissions, with a complete off-chain audit trail.

{{ icon('activity', 'icon-lg') }}

Integrity-first design

Readings are append-only. Back-dated entries and duplicate dates are rejected to preserve the meter chain.

How it works

Four steps from meter to proof

A simple resident experience backed by a rigorous integrity model.

Read the meter

Enter the current cumulative meter value. The previous value is pulled from the last reading.

Calculate & hash

Consumption is calculated and a canonical record digest is created with keccak256.

Anchor on Sepolia

The digest, consumption in millilitres, sequence and reading date are stored by the smart contract.

Verify anytime

Recompute the digest from the database and compare it with the immutable on-chain proof.
